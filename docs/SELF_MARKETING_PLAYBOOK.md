## 1. Strategy & Philosophy

As an early-stage founder with limited budget, **you cannot afford to blast generic mass emails to thousands of unverified people**. Mass spam leads to domain blacklisting, wasted effort, and zero revenue.

Instead, this playbook executes a high-conviction, evidence-based approach:
1. **Target 5 to 10 highly relevant prospects per day.**
2. **Review every prospect's actual website and business model.**
3. **Offer an introductory value-first audit or prototype dashboard (low friction, high trust).**
4. **Follow up professionally and track genuine conversion stages.**

## 2. The 3 Genuinely Sellable Offers

Do not pitch a vague "AI platform" to small businesses that do not understand SaaS. Pitch specific, painful operational problems that business owners actively struggle with:

### Offer A: The "Messy Spreadsheet to Automated Dashboard" Overhaul
* **Who it is for:** Dental clinics, medical practices, real estate brokers, accounting firms, and local agencies.
* **Their pain:** Practice owners or office managers spend 5-10 hours every week manually copying numbers between booking spreadsheets, billing sheets, and doctor schedules, leading to formulas breaking and lost revenue.
* **Your offer:**
  *"I will consolidate your scattered booking and revenue spreadsheets into a single, clean, automated Excel or Power BI dashboard with automated refresh and error checking."*
* **Introductory Price:** **$150 – $350** (or local equivalent PKR 40,000 – 95,000) for a 3-page interactive dashboard delivered within 48 hours.

### Offer B: Executive Power BI Sales & KPI Visualizer
* **Who it is for:** E-commerce stores, logistics companies, digital marketing agencies, and distributors.
* **Their pain:** They have data across Shopify, Meta Ads, and Excel, but the CEO has no daily visual summary of customer acquisition cost (CAC), gross margin, and stock turnover.
* **Your offer:**
  *"A custom interactive Power BI executive dashboard connecting your sales data, tracking daily KPIs, and automatically highlighting low-performing products."*
* **Introductory Price:** **$350 – $750** (or PKR 95,000 – 200,000).

### Offer C: Supervised B2B Lead List Research & Enrichment
* **Who it is for:** B2B consultants, software development agencies, and overseas outsourcing firms.
* **Their pain:** They want to reach CEOs or clinic owners in the UK, UAE, or US, but buying cheap lead lists gives them 50% dead emails and generic info.
* **Your offer:**
  *"I will research 100 verified decision-maker prospects in your exact niche using our custom search engine, complete with verified website evidence, mobile/WhatsApp contacts, and tailored opening icebreakers."*
* **Introductory Price:** **$150 per 100 verified enriched leads**.

## 3. Step-by-Step Daily Execution Workflow

### Step 1: Discover High-Fit Real Prospects
1. Log in to your platform at `http://localhost:3000/login`.
2. Navigate to **Lead Discovery** (`/app/leads`).
3. Set your target criteria:
   - **Keyword:** `dental clinic` (or `real estate agency`, `digital marketing`)
   - **City:** `Lahore` (or `Dubai`, `London`, `Karachi`)
   - **Country:** `Pakistan` (or `United Arab Emirates`, `United Kingdom`)
   - **Count:** `10`
4. Click **Search Google Live**.
5. The system queries Google and returns verified businesses with live websites and source URLs.

### Step 2: Quality Inspection & Evidence Check
1. Click the **Website** link of each result to inspect their actual public presence.
2. Confirm:
   - Is this an active, operational business?
   - Do they list a phone number or contact email?
   - Do they have multiple doctors/staff or services that indicate operational complexity?
3. Click **Save to CRM** on the 5 to 10 best matching prospects.

### Step 3: Generate Evidence-Based Pitch Draft
1. Navigate to **AI Research Studio** (`/app/research`) or open the lead in **CRM** (`/app/crm`).
2. Input the target company domain.
3. Review the extracted evidence:
   - Verified facts (from meta description and website)
   - Identified operational pain points
   - Suggested outreach draft

### Step 4: Choose Channel & Execute Approved Outreach

#### Option 1: WhatsApp Web Manual Outreach (Fastest response rate for local SMBs & clinics)
1. Go to **WhatsApp Companion** (`/app/whatsapp`).
2. Select the saved lead from the dropdown.
3. Choose template: **"Excel & Analytics Workflow Review"**.
4. The system automatically inserts their business name and local context.
5. Review and customize the wording.
6. Click **Open in WhatsApp Web**.
7. WhatsApp Web opens in your browser with the message pre-filled.
8. Review one final time and press Send.

#### Option 2: Direct Email Outreach
If you have connected Gmail, send the reviewed email draft from your account. If not, copy the draft into your standard email client and send it directly.

## 5. Follow-Up Cadence (Where 80% of Deals Are Won)

Most business owners do not reply on the first message not because they are uninterested, but because they are busy.

* **Day 1:** Initial Outreach (Value-first offer).
* **Day 4 (Follow-up 1):**
  > *"Hi [Name], following up briefly on my note from Tuesday. Even if you are not looking to overhaul your reporting right now, happy to share a free template you can use internally. Let me know if that would be helpful!"*
* **Day 8 (Follow-up 2 - Soft Close):**
  > *"Hi [Name], wanted to check in one last time. If reporting automation isn't a priority for [Clinic/Company] this month, no worries at all. Feel free to keep my contact for future reference if spreadsheet bottlenecks arise."*

## 6. Real CRM Stage Progression

Track every prospect honestly in `/app/crm`:
* **New Lead:** Prospect discovered and saved with source URL.
* **Contacted:** Outreach message approved and sent.
* **Replied:** Genuine prospect response received.
* **Meeting:** Scheduled a 15-minute phone or Zoom call to review their current spreadsheet.
* **Proposal:** Sent quote (e.g., $250 for custom dashboard).
* **Won:** Contract agreed and payment received.

Never mark a lead as "Won" or "Replied" without actual evidence.
