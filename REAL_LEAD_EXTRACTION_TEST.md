## 1. Compliance Directives & Search Rules

1. **Strict 4-Field Qualification Guarantee:**
   A business entity is ONLY qualified and saved if and only if all four essential data points are discovered and verified:
   - **Business Name** (Real trade name, cleaned of SEO taglines)
   - **Official Website** (Clickable, valid HTTP/HTTPS URL)
   - **Direct Business Email** (Format validated against standard RFC syntax, domain checked, non-image)
   - **Phone Number** (Format checked with country/region dial code)

2. **Total Exclusion of Google Maps / Google Places:**
   - All legacy `/places` queries to Google Maps endpoints are completely stripped from `app/api/leads/search/route.ts` and `backend/app/services/lead_service.py`.
   - Lead acquisition executes exclusively via **Google Organic Search**, **Direct Official Websites**, **Facebook Business**, **Instagram Business**, **LinkedIn Company Directory**, and **Commercial Industry Portals**.

3. **Incomplete Leads Handling:**
   - Incomplete leads lacking any of the 4 required fields are **silently excluded** in the backend processing pipeline.
   - **Zero incomplete records** are presented to the user, ensuring the UI remains pristine, professional, and high-value.

4. **Pakistan Standard Time (PKT) Timestamps:**
   - All leads are assigned `saved_date` and `saved_time` in `Asia/Karachi` (PKT, UTC+5).
   - Export operations stamp `export_date` and `export_time` in PKT.
